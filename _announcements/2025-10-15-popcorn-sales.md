🍿 **It's that time of year again - Popcorn Sales have begun!**

Pack 3472's annual popcorn fundraiser is our biggest source of funding for campouts, activities, and equipment. Every scout can participate and earn prizes while supporting the pack!

## How It Works

1. **Get Your Sales Kit**: Pick up from the committee chair at the next pack meeting
2. **Sell Popcorn**: Door-to-door, at family events, to coworkers, etc.
3. **Submit Orders**: Online or paper forms due November 15th
4. **Deliver Product**: Mid-December pickup and delivery to customers
5. **Earn Prizes**: Individual prizes PLUS pack earns 30% of all sales!

## Where Money Goes

Funds from popcorn sales support:
- Camping trips and outdoor adventures
- Pack meeting supplies and activities  
- Rank advancement materials
- Equipment and gear
- Scholarship assistance for scouts in need

## Top Sellers Win Big!

Scouts selling $1000+ earn **BONUS PRIZES** including:
- Special activity passes
- Exclusive camping gear
- Scout shop gift cards
- Recognition at our Blue & Gold Banquet

## Safety Reminders

- Youth must be accompanied by an adult when selling door-to-door
- Wear your scout uniform or Pack 3472 shirt
- Be polite and respectful
- Never enter a stranger's home
