Join us for our November pack meeting! This month we'll be focusing on outdoor skills and preparing for our upcoming camping trip.

## What to Expect

- **Opening Ceremony**: Flag presentation and Scout Oath/Law
- **Rank Advancements**: Congratulations to scouts earning new ranks!
- **Outdoor Skills Workshop**: Learn fire safety, knot tying, and compass navigation
- **Den Time**: Break into dens for age-specific activities
- **Pack Business**: Important announcements about upcoming events
- **Closing**: Awards, prizes, and snacks

## What to Bring

- Scout uniform (Class A or Class B)
- Scout handbook
- Positive attitude and enthusiasm!

## Parents

Parents are encouraged to stay for the meeting. We'll have coffee and information about upcoming volunteer opportunities.

**Questions?** Contact our Cubmaster at cubmaster@pack3472.org
