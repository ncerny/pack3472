Get ready for the annual Pinewood Derby race! Join us for a car-building workshop where scouts and families can work together to create their racing masterpieces.

## Workshop Details

Experienced pack leaders and parent volunteers will be on hand to help with:

- Car design ideas and templates
- Safe use of tools (saws, sanders, drills)
- Weight placement for optimal speed
- Painting and decoration techniques
- Testing on our practice track

## What's Provided

- Workbenches and tools
- Sandpaper and files
- Basic paints and brushes
- Weights and placement guides
- Safety equipment
- Snacks and drinks

## What to Bring

- Your official Pinewood Derby kit (available at scout shop or online)
- Design ideas or sketches
- Special decorations if desired (stickers, decals, etc.)
- Safety glasses if you have them

## Derby Race Day

The official Pinewood Derby race will be held on **January 11th**. This workshop helps you get started, but you'll finish your car at home before race day.

## For Parents

Adult supervision is required for all tool use. This is a great opportunity to work alongside your scout and create memories together!

**Cost**: FREE (but you must provide your own Pinewood Derby kit - about $5-7)

Can't make the workshop? No problem - you can still build at home and participate in the race!
