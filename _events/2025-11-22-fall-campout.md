Pack 3472's annual fall campout is coming up! Join us for a weekend of camping, hiking, campfires, and outdoor adventures at beautiful Camp Illinek.

## Event Details

**Check-in**: Friday, November 22nd at 5:00 PM  
**Check-out**: Sunday, November 24th at 2:00 PM  
**Location**: Camp Illinek, 8806 Illinek Rd, Springfield, IL 62707

## Activities Include

- 🏕️ Tent camping under the stars
- 🔥 Campfire cooking and Dutch oven meals
- 🥾 Guided nature hikes on scenic trails
- 🎯 Archery and BB gun shooting ranges
- 🎨 Nature crafts and outdoor skills
- ⚽ Field games and competitions
- 🌙 Evening campfire with songs and skits
- ⛪ Sunday morning Scout's Own service

## Cost

- **Scouts**: $35 per person
- **Adults**: $25 per person
- **Family Maximum**: $125
- Price includes all meals from Friday dinner through Sunday lunch

## What to Bring

### Essential Gear
- Tent (or share with another family)
- Sleeping bag rated for 40°F or below
- Sleeping pad or air mattress
- Pillow
- Flashlight or headlamp with extra batteries

### Clothing
- Warm layers (it gets cold at night!)
- Rain jacket
- Extra socks and underwear
- Sturdy hiking boots or shoes
- Hat and gloves
- Scout uniform for ceremonies

### Personal Items
- Toiletries and towel
- Water bottle
- Sunscreen and bug spray
- Any medications
- Camp chair (optional)

### Do NOT Bring
- Electronics (phones ok for parents, but discouraged for scouts)
- Fireworks or weapons
- Pets
- Expensive items

## Medical Forms Required

All participants must have a current BSA Annual Health & Medical Record (Parts A & B) on file. Forms will be collected at check-in.
