Pack 3472 is partnering with the Springfield Food Bank for a morning of service! This is a great opportunity for scouts to give back to our community and work on their service requirements.

## What We'll Do

Scouts will help sort and package food donations for local families in need. Tasks are age-appropriate and supervised by trained volunteers.

- Sort canned goods and dry goods
- Pack food boxes for distribution
- Organize the donation storage area
- Learn about food insecurity in our community

## Who Can Participate

All ranks welcome! Lions and Tigers must be accompanied by an adult partner. Bears, Webelos, and AOL scouts may work independently under supervision.

## What to Wear

- Scout uniform or Pack 3472 t-shirt
- Comfortable closed-toe shoes (no sandals)
- Clothes that can get dusty

## Service Hours

Scouts will earn **3 community service hours** for participating. Bring your scout handbook for recording.

## Parent Volunteers Needed

We need 4-5 adult volunteers to help supervise. Background checks required for all volunteers (already on file for registered pack leaders).

**RSVP by November 6th** so we can confirm our group size with the food bank.

Together we can make a difference! 🤝
