How to subscribe

Google Calendar

  1. Click "Add to Google Calendar" above
  2. Sign in to your Google account if prompted
  3. Click "Add calendar" to confirm
  4. Pack 3472 events will appear in your calendar automatically!

Apple Calendar (iPhone/iPad/Mac)

  1. Click "Add to Apple Calendar" above
  2. When prompted, tap/click "Subscribe"
  3. Events will sync automatically to your device

Outlook or Other Calendar Apps

  1. Click "Download iCal File" above
  2. Open the downloaded .ics file
  3. Your calendar app will import the subscription
  4. Choose to subscribe (not import) for automatic updates
