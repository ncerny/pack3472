## Join Pack 3472 Cub Scouts!

We're excited that you're interested in joining Pack 3472! Cub Scouting is open to boys and girls in kindergarten through 5th grade. No prior scouting experience is necessary.

### Who Can Join?

**Kindergarten through 5th Grade** (ages 5-10)

- **Lions** - Kindergarten
- **Tigers** - 1st Grade
- **Wolves** - 2nd Grade
- **Bears** - 3rd Grade
- **Webelos** - 4th Grade
- **Arrow of Light** - 5th Grade

You can join at any time during the school year! We accept new scouts year-round and will help you get caught up with your den.

## What to Expect

### First Meeting

Your scout will:

- Meet their Den Leader and den members
- Participate in activities and games
- Learn about their rank requirements
- Have fun!

Parents are welcome and encouraged to stay, especially for younger scouts (Lions and Tigers require adult partners).

### Weekly Den Meetings

- Small group meetings (6-8 scouts)
- Age-appropriate activities and adventures
- Work toward rank advancement
- Build friendships and skills
- Duration: 60-90 minutes

### Monthly Pack Meetings

- Whole pack gathering
- Games, ceremonies, and recognition
- Guest speakers and demonstrations
- Family-friendly fun
- Duration: 90 minutes

### Special Events

Throughout the year, pack events include:

- Campouts and outdoor adventures
- Pinewood Derby car race
- Blue & Gold Banquet
- Service projects
- Field trips
- Family events

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When can my child join?

You can join anytime during the year! While fall is our primary recruiting season, we accept new members year-round.

### Do you accept boys and girls?

Yes! Cub Scouting is open to all youth in grades K-5, regardless of gender.

### What if my child has special needs?

We welcome all scouts! BSA provides accommodations and support for scouts with disabilities. Please talk with our leadership about your child's specific needs.

### Do parents need to participate?

Parent involvement is encouraged! Lions and Tigers require an adult partner. For older scouts, parents are welcome but not required to attend meetings. We do need adult volunteers for events and leadership positions.

### What if we can't afford the fees?

Financial assistance is available. Please contact our Cubmaster or Committee Chair confidentially. No child is turned away due to inability to pay.

### Can my child join if we miss the beginning of the year?

Absolutely! New scouts are welcome at any time. We'll help your scout catch up with their den's progress.

### What about religious requirements?

The Scout Oath includes "duty to God," but BSA welcomes families of all faiths. Religious awards are available for many different religions, and participation is optional.

### How much time commitment is required?

- Den meetings: 60-90 minutes weekly
- Pack meetings: 90 minutes monthly
- Special events: Varies (campouts are typically weekends)
- Family participation: As much as you want!

### Is Cub Scouting only about camping?

No! While outdoor activities are important, Cub Scouts do much more: STEM projects, service work, sports, arts & crafts, community visits, and character development.
